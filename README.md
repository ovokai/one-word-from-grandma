The word my grandma would text about AI:

**ChatGTP**

She has heard of ChatGPT. She talks about it all the time. She has just
never, not once, spelled it in the right order — and honestly, in her
phone it will live as ChatGTP forever.

`main.ts` (Node, standard library only) writes the word to
`/output/entry.txt`.
